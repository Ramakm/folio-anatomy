Plate I · Scaled dot-product attention

Attention, as an assay bench.

A query probe reads every key on the bench. A sealed reservoir holds exactly one unit of attention and pours it out by the softmax. The output settles into a core sample whose strata are the weights.

Key to the plate

Parts and what they compute

  1. 1Query carriageRides the rail to the word doing the looking. Carries q = x WQ.
  2. 2Key dialsOne per word. The needle reads q·k / √dk; the red tick is zero.
  3. 3Glass columnsThe fluid each word receives. Its height is the weight aj, graduated in tenths.
  4. 4Unit reservoirHolds one unit, sealed. Softmax can only share it out, so the columns always sum to 1.
  5. 5Stopcocks and shuttersThe causal mask. Words after the query are shut off and capped.
  6. 6Core-sample trayThe output Σ aj vj, laid down in word order. Stratum length is weight.
  7. 7Head selectorFour heads, four learned habits. Same words, different WQ, WK.
  8. 8Temperature knobDivides every score before softmax. Low pours into one column, high spreads it.
  9. 9Mask leverCausal (decoder) or open (encoder) attention.

Wall text I

Reading the plate, left to right

The bench does the four steps of attention in the order the formula writes them. Every number below is read from the same model that moves the needles and pours the fluid, so they change together.

i.

Compare

sj = q · kj

The carriage 1 holds the query. Each key dial 2 measures how far its word's key points the same way.

ii.

Scale

sj / √dk  =  sj / 2.83

A dot product of eight terms grows with the dimension. Dividing by √8 keeps the dials on their scale, so softmax does not saturate.

iii.

Normalise

aj = ezj / Σ ez

The reservoir 4 holds one unit. Open stopcocks 5 share it in proportion to ez; shut ones get nothing.

iv.

Blend

o = Σ aj vj

The tray 6 lays the fluid down word by word. The output is mostly the longest stratum, with a trace of the rest.

Wall text III

Every query at once

The plate shows one row of the attention matrix. Here is the whole matrix for the current head: rows are the word looking, columns the word looked at. The hatched triangle is the mask.

Four heads, four habits

A transformer layer runs many heads side by side on the same words. Each learns its own projections, so each pours the same unit of attention to different places. The heads here are set by hand to show patterns that have been found in trained models.

Wall text IV

About this specimen

What is exact

The arithmetic. Each word is an 11-number vector (bias, five word-class features, four positional features). Each head multiplies it by its own WQ and WK to get eight-number queries and keys, and the plate runs softmax(qKᵀ/√8 / T) on them, live.

What is set by hand

The weights. A trained model learns WQ and WK from data; these were written to reproduce four documented behaviours: coreference, previous-token heads, attention sinks on the first token, and a verb lookup.

What is left out

Value vectors are shown as strata, not numbers. A real layer also has many heads in parallel, an output projection, residual connections and layer normalisation. The plate is one head, one row at a time.
