How a plate is made

Four phases, from brief to proof

The skill follows the same method for every idea, so each plate is accurate before it is beautiful.

i.

Brief

Write the model first and test it. Choose an instrument, then map each term of the idea to a part of it.

ii.

Bench

Lay the parts out in depth bands, engrave the static geometry once and redraw only the moving parts.

iii.

Mount

Add the title block, the numbered key, the controls and four wall texts: reading, ledger, wider view and an honest note.

iv.

Proof

Screenshot it in light, dark and on a phone, zoom in on the joints, and check every number against the model.
